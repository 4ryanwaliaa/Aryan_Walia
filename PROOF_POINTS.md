# Keeping portfolio claims current

Review product descriptions, links, availability wording and service promises before each release and periodically thereafter. The portfolio deliberately avoids a live count of MyPentest checks or passing tests: those figures change and should only be published with a dated, reproducible source. Verify a test count by running the current scanner's test suite, and distinguish a local result from the deployed service.

- [MyPentest product](https://bugsnaps.in/mypentest): check feature, pricing and report claims against the product and scanner source.
- [MyRecon source and documentation](https://github.com/4ryanwalia/Myrecon): check supported search modes, platform coverage and limits; interfaces do not necessarily offer identical coverage.
- [Wake Me on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=makeme.aryan.makeme): check the listing and current availability.
- [BugSnaps](https://bugsnaps.in): check current services and any turnaround or retest promises.
